## 2) Feature-list ad (blog / landing page)

**LandGPS — the Handy-GPS-style land measurement app for farmers, landowners and agronomists**

- **Measure by walking** — open the map, walk around your land, tap *Add point* at each corner (or let it auto-mark every 10 m). No tapes, no trigonometry.
- **Live boundary & area** — see your polygon drawn as you go with live area and perimeter in m², hectares or acres.
- **Exact coordinates** — every waypoint's lat/long is saved; view or share the full coordinate list.
- **KML in & out** — import boundaries from Google Earth / survey files (KML or KMZ), and export any plot to KML.
- **Crop health from space** — choose a growing period and LandGPS pulls Sentinel-2 satellite imagery via Google Earth Engine to compute **NDVI, NDRE, EVI and SAVI** for your exact field boundary.
- **Clear verdict** — color-mapped NDVI map of your plot plus an overall **Healthy / Moderate / Stressed** classification, with per-index stats.
- **Works on-device** — your plots are saved locally, no account required.

## 3) App Store / Play Store description

**LandGPS – Measure Land & Check Crop Health**

Measure any piece of land by simply walking around it. LandGPS works like a
handheld GPS survey app (think Handy GPS) right on your phone: mark waypoints
as you walk, and the app draws your boundary, computes the area and perimeter,
and keeps the exact GPS coordinates of every point.

Need the boundary you already drew in Google Earth? Import it from KML or KMZ,
or export any plot back to KML for sharing.

Then let satellites do the farming analysis. Select a growing period and
LandGPS uses Google Earth Engine to analyze Sentinel-2 imagery over your exact
field boundary — returning a crop health report with NDVI, NDRE, EVI and SAVI
indices, a color-coded health map of your plot, and an overall
Healthy / Moderate / Stressed rating.

Perfect for:
- Farmers checking field health field-by-field
- Landowners verifying plot boundaries and size
- Agronomists and advisors preparing crop reports
- Surveyors doing quick on-site area checks

Download LandGPS and turn every walk around your land into data you can use.
