📋 How to get Survey / Khata / Owner details
  1. Open the official portal for your state — button below.
  2. Select District → Tehsil → Village on the portal.
  3. Open the Khatauni (record of rights / jamabandi).
  4. Find your Khata number and the Khasra / Survey number for this plot.
  5. Copy the Owner name from the khatauni entry.
  6. Enter them below and press Save — they auto-fill for this location next time.

📁 My Land Records (auto-match)
Upload your own records CSV — clicking a field then shows the matching row automatically. Columns: lat, lng, survey, khata, owner, motor, pipeline, electricity, village, district, state, pincode
No coordinates? No problem — rows with village + district are matched by name against the clicked field's location.
💡 Use the Download sample CSV template button below — open it in Excel/Google Sheets, replace the example rows with your own plots, and save back as CSV.
❓ How to build this CSV from official records
1. Open your state's official land portal (button above) — e.g. UP Bhulekh.
2. Select District → Tehsil → Village.
3. Open the Khatauni → note the Khata number, Khasra/Survey number and Owner name.
4. Open a spreadsheet (Excel / Google Sheets).
5. Make a header row: lat, lng, survey, khata, owner, motor, pipeline, electricity, village, district, state, pincode
6. Add one row per plot — only village or district is required; coordinates are optional.
7. Save as CSV (UTF-8) and upload it here — clicking a field now shows its row automatically.
